### Situation: Choosing a Group Vacation Destination
Situation: You and your group of friends are deciding on a destination for a vacation trip. There are three options:
- Vung Tau (convenient for a short trip)
- Da Lat (cool mountain climate and scenic nature)
- Can Tho (rich Mekong Delta river culture)
Which destination would you choose and why?
