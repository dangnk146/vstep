### Situation: Choosing Accommodation for a First-Year University Student
Situation: Your cousin has just passed the university entrance examination and is looking for a place to live. There are three options:
- Living in the university dormitory
- Sharing a rented room or house with friends
- Renting a private studio apartment
Which option would you advise them to choose and why?

| Option | 💡 Cách suy luận & Mục tiêu | Lý do & Lập luận (Reasons) |
