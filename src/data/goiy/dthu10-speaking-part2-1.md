### Situation: Free Time Activities
You have some free time this weekend. There are three options for you to spend your leisure time:
- Meeting up with friends
- Going shopping
- Relaxing alone at a coffee shop
Which option do you choose and why?

---

### Bảng dàn ý Part 2

| Option | Lý do |
|---|---|
| **Chọn: Meeting up with friends** | - Mental health & Friendship:<br>+ spend quality time catching up, laughing, and sharing stories after a busy week<br>+ strengthen bonds with close friends and reduce stress<br>+ feel joyful and energetic instead of sitting bored at home<br><br>- Fun & Flexible:<br>+ can drink coffee, eat street food, or walk in the park together without spending much money |
| **Không chọn:** | - Going shopping: shopping malls on weekends are crowded and noisy; easy to overspend money on clothes I don't really need.<br>- Relaxing alone at a coffee shop: can be quite lonely on a weekend; I can relax alone in my bedroom anytime during the week. |
