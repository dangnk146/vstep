# Topic 1: Saving Money

1. Do you often save money? Why or why not?
2. Have you ever wasted your money on unnecessary things?
3. What is the best way for young people to save money?

---

### Bảng dàn ý Part 1

| # | Answer (Lựa chọn & Trả lời trực diện) | Reason (Lý do A-R-E) | Example (Ví dụ thực tế) |

| **1** | Yes, I try to save money regularly<br>- Every month | - Financial security and peace of mind:<br>• prepare for unexpected emergencies (medical bills, bike repair)<br>• save for future plans like taking English courses or travelling | • Putting aside 15–20% of my monthly allowance or income into a bank savings account |
| **2** | Yes, sometimes in the past<br>- Especially on impulse shopping | - Emotional spending when stressed:<br>• bought trendy clothes on sales that I rarely wore<br>• wasted money on expensive snacks and milk tea | • Buying an expensive jacket online during Black Friday and only wearing it once |
