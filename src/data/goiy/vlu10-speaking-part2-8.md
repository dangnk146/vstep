### Situation: Career Choice in Health and Wellness
Your friend has a strong passion for healthcare and wellness and is deciding which career path to pursue. There are three options:
- A personal fitness trainer
- A community healthcare worker
- A professional nutritionist
Which career would you advise your friend to choose and why?
