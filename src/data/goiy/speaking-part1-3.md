# Topic 3: Taking Breaks

1. Do you think taking breaks during work or study is important?
2. Do you prefer taking one long break or several short breaks?
3. What do you usually do when you take a break?

---

### Bảng dàn ý Part 1

| # | Answer (Lựa chọn & Trả lời trực diện) | Reason (Lý do A-R-E) | Example (Ví dụ thực tế) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Extremely important<br>- Essential for both body and mind | - Mental & physical health:<br>• continuous staring at screens causes eye strain, headaches, and fatigue<br>• resting for a few minutes refreshes the mind and improves study efficiency | • Taking a 5-minute break after every 45 minutes of intense revision |
| **2** | Several short breaks throughout the day<br>- Rather than one long break | - Maintain steady energy and momentum:<br>• keeps concentration sharp without getting tired<br>• a long break makes me too lazy and hard to get back to work | • Taking two or three 10-minute breaks in the morning to walk around and stretch |
| **3** | Stretch body, drink water, and rest my eyes | - Physical relief and relaxation:<br>• stand up to relieve back stiffness, look out the window at green trees<br>• drink water or have a light healthy snack | • Standing up from the computer desk, walking around, and making a cup of tea |
