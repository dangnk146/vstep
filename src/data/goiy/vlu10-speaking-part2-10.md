### Situation: Choosing a Pet for a Child
A family with a ten-year-old child wants to adopt a household pet to teach the child responsibility and provide companionship. Three animals are being considered: a dog, a cat, or a goldfish. Which pet would you recommend for the family, and why?

| Option | Lý do |
