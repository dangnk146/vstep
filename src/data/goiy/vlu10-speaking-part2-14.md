### Situation: Choosing an Activity While Babysitting a Nephew
You have agreed to babysit your young nephew for your sister this Saturday afternoon. There are three options for where you can take him:
- Taking him to a place he personally loves
- Taking him to an indoor video game center
- Taking him to an international educational learning center
Which option do you choose and why?

---

### Bảng dàn ý Part 2

| Option | Lý do |
|---|---|
| **Chọn: A place he personally loves** | - Joy & Family bonding:<br>+ respect his choice: he will be thrilled, happy, and cooperate willingly<br>+ no tantrums, tears, or stubbornness; babysitting becomes fun and easy<br>+ strengthen the bond between aunt/uncle and nephew<br><br>- Meaningful weekend:<br>+ Saturday is for playing and having fun after a long week of school |
| **Không chọn:** | - An indoor video game center: too noisy, bad for children's eyes, and easy for them to get addicted or throw tantrums when leaving.<br>- An international learning center: feels like extra school on a weekend; children will feel bored and pressured. |
