### Situation: Choosing a Teaching Job
Situation: You have just graduated from a pedagogical university and are deciding where to teach. There are three options:
- A public school near home
- An international school far from home
- A private school far from home
Which option would you choose and why?

---

### Bảng dàn ý Part 2

| Option | 💡 Cách suy luận & Mục tiêu | Lý do & Lập luận (Reasons) |
