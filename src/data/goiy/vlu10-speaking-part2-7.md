### Situation: Choosing a Farewell Gift for a Colleague
A close colleague in your department is leaving for a new job opportunity, and you want to give him/her a memorable farewell gift. There are three options:
- A personalized hand-made greeting card
- A framed photo of you and your colleague together
- A special dinner treat at a good restaurant
Which option do you think is the best choice and why?

---

### Bảng dàn ý Part 2

| Option | Lý do |
|---|---|
| **Chọn: A framed photo of us together** | - Emotional value & Good memories:<br>+ captures a real, joyful moment of our friendship at work<br>+ can place it on his/her new work desk or at home<br>+ every time he/she looks at it, he/she will remember our good times and smile<br><br>- Meaningful & Affordable:<br>+ easy to prepare, affordable, but lasts for many years |
| **Không chọn:** | - A hand-made card: sweet words, but usually gets forgotten inside a book or drawer.<br>- A dinner treat: enjoyable, but once the meal ends, nothing physical is left as a reminder; can also be quite expensive. |
