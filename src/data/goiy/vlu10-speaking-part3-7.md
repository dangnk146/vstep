**Topic:** Practical solutions to tackle obesity in modern society
- Promoting nutrition education and balanced diets in schools
- Encouraging daily physical exercise through community sports facilities
- Imposing strict regulations on advertising junk food to children
- [Your own ideas]

**Follow-up questions:**
1. Who should take greater responsibility for the growing rate of childhood obesity: parents or fast food companies?
2. How does obesity negatively affect an individual's physical health and mental self-esteem?
3. What initiatives can modern companies implement in the workplace to keep their employees active and healthy?

| **Ý 1** | Nutrition education and balanced diets in schools | - Build healthy eating habits early:<br>• teach pupils about vitamins, calories, and benefits of greens<br>• replace greasy cafeteria food with fresh vegetables, fruit, and lean meat | • School cafeterias serving steamed chicken and broccoli instead of sausages and French fries |
| **Ý 2** | Community sports facilities | - Encourage everyone to work out daily:<br>• build free outdoor exercise machines, jogging paths, and sports courts in parks<br>• easily accessible for low-income families and seniors to stay active | • Public parks with free exercise machines crowded with people every morning and evening |
| **Ý 3** | Restricting junk food ads to children | - Reduce tempting marketing on kids:<br>• stop colorful cartoon commercials promoting soda, chips, and candies<br>• helps children ask for fewer sugary snacks when shopping | • Banning fast food and soda commercials during children's cartoon hours on TV |
| **Q1** | Parents hold the primary responsibility | - Parents choose what food to buy, cook home meals, and control snack habits<br>- Fast food companies run for business, but parents decide what their kids eat daily | • When parents cook healthy meals at home, children rarely crave junk food |
| **Q2** | Serious disease risks and low self-confidence | - Physical: causes high blood pressure, diabetes, heart disease, and joint pain<br>- Mental: feel insecure about body shape, suffer from teasing, and feel shy in social situations | • Overweight students feeling too self-conscious to join school swimming or running events |
| **Q3** | Free gym passes and healthy office snacks | - Provide gym vouchers or hold step-counting challenges among departments<br>- Replace sugary snacks and soda in office pantries with fresh fruit, nuts, and filtered water | • A company offering a monthly step challenge with small prizes for active employees |
