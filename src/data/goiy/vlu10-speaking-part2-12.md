### Situation: Promoting Science Interest among Students
A high school wants to inspire its students to develop a strong passion for science and technology. Three initiatives are proposed: inviting a guest scientist to give an inspiring talk, donating science books to the school library, or organizing interactive science games and competitions. Which option do you think is the most effective, and why?
