### Situation: Promoting Science Interest among Students
A high school wants to inspire its students to develop a strong passion for science and technology. Three initiatives are proposed: inviting a guest scientist to give an inspiring talk, donating science books to the school library, or organizing interactive science games and competitions. Which option do you think is the most effective, and why?

---

### Bảng dàn ý Part 2

| Option | Lý do |
|---|---|
| **Chọn: Interactive science games & competitions** | - Hands-on practice & Curiosity:<br>+ learn by doing: build mini robots, launch water rockets, do fun chemistry experiments<br>+ see real science in action, sparking excitement and imagination<br><br>- Teamwork & Fun:<br>+ work in teams with classmates, compete for prizes, and have fun together<br>+ memorable experience that makes science feel approachable and cool |
| **Không chọn:** | - Inviting a guest scientist: long academic speeches can be dry and theoretical; students easily lose focus or fall asleep.<br>- Donating science books: students already read too many school textbooks; thick science books will just sit untouched on library shelves. |
