**Topic:** Causes and solutions for student misbehavior in schools
- Lack of parental guidance and attention
- Peer pressure and negative influence from friends
- Negative influence of violent media and online content
- [Your own ideas]

**Follow-up questions:**
1. How can parents and educators effectively correct students' misbehavior without using physical punishment?
2. Should schools permanently expel students who repeatedly break discipline rules?
3. In what ways can a positive and supportive school environment encourage good student behavior?

| **Ý 1** | Lack of parental guidance and attention | - Parents too busy with work:<br>• lack time to talk, listen, and guide their children<br>• kids feel lonely and act out to get attention from adults | • Kids whose parents work late every night skipping homework and misbehaving |
| **Ý 2** | Peer pressure and negative influence from friends | - Wanting to fit into a friend group:<br>• teenagers easily copy bad habits (smoking, swearing, skipping classes)<br>• afraid of being bullied or left out by peers | • A student skipping classes because the "popular" group in class told him to |
| **Ý 3** | Violent media and online content | - Excessive exposure to violent content:<br>• watching violent action films or playing fighting games for hours<br>• children copy aggressive actions and shout at classmates | • Boys hitting classmates after playing aggressive shooting video games |
| **Q1** | Calm communication and positive discipline | - Listen to understand their feelings and problems<br>- Explain clearly why the behavior is bad and set fair boundaries with patience | • A teacher having a private, gentle conversation with a student after class |
| **Q2** | Only as the very last option | - Expelling pushes them into society with no guidance, making them worse<br>- Schools should offer psychological counseling and chances to correct mistakes | • Giving temporary suspension and community cleaning service before expelling |
| **Q3** | Praise good efforts and organize fun extracurricular clubs | - When students feel loved, respected, and listened to, they behave nicely<br>- Sports and music clubs help students burn excess energy and make good friends | • Praising students who help others and holding football matches between classes |
