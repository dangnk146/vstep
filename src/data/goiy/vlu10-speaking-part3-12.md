| **Ý 1** | Expressing emotions and building friendly connection | - Show warmth and sincerity:<br>• gentle smiles, friendly eye contact, and open postures make others feel comfortable<br>• helps build trust and make new friends quickly | • Giving a warm smile and a polite handshake when greeting a new coworker |
| **Ý 2** | Clarifying spoken words and intentions | - Make messages clearer and easier to understand:<br>• hand gestures help describe size, direction, and emphasize important points<br>• prevents confusion and keeps listeners engaged | • Using hand movements during a presentation to highlight key points |
| **Ý 3** | Potential misunderstandings due to cultural differences | - Gestures can mean different things in different countries:<br>• a gesture considered polite in one country might be offensive in another | • Direct eye contact shows confidence in Western cultures, but may seem disrespectful to traditional elders in some Asian cultures |
| **Q1** | Lack of exact details and easy to misinterpret | - Body language cannot convey exact numbers, agreements, or technical facts<br>- People can easily misread nervous habits (like leg shaking) as lying or impoliteness | • You cannot sign a job contract or negotiate prices using only gestures |
| **Q2** | Face-to-face conversations<br>- Bring real warmth and true connection | - Can see real smiles, hear tone of voice, and observe immediate reactions<br>- Prevents cold text misunderstandings | • Talking in person over coffee to resolve a misunderstanding with a friend |
| **Q3** | Practice good posture, eye contact, and active listening | - Sit up straight, avoid crossing arms or looking at your phone during meetings<br>- Practice speaking in front of a mirror to look confident and friendly | • Practicing job interview answers in front of a mirror to maintain steady eye contact |
