### Situation: Measures to Reduce Traffic Accidents
The city transport authority is seeking effective measures to reduce traffic accidents and improve road safety. Three solutions are being proposed: enforcing stricter traffic laws and heavier penalties, widening roads and expanding transportation infrastructure, or adjusting the legal minimum driving age. Which solution do you think is the most effective, and why?

---

### Bảng dàn ý Part 2

| Option | Lý do |
|---|---|
| **Chọn: Stricter traffic laws & heavier penalties** | - Immediate impact on road behavior:<br>+ heavy fines and license bans make drivers think twice before speeding or running red lights<br>+ strictly penalize drunk driving, saving lives on the road immediately<br><br>- Fast and cost-effective:<br>+ traffic police and road cameras can enforce rules right away without huge costs<br>+ builds respect for the law and long-term safe driving habits |
| **Không chọn:** | - Widening roads: takes many years of construction, causes massive traffic jams while building, and costs huge amounts of public money.<br>- Adjusting minimum driving age: age is not the main issue; adults still cause accidents if they drive carelessly or drink alcohol. |
