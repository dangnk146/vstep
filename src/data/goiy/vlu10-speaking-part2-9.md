### Situation: Organizing a Freshmen Welcome Event
Your university student club is organizing a welcome party for new freshmen. Three main activities are being considered: an arts and crafts workshop, a live music performance, or an international food fair. Which activity do you think would be the best choice to welcome freshmen, and why?
