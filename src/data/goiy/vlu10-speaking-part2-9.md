### Situation: Organizing a Freshmen Welcome Event
Your university student club is organizing a welcome party for new freshmen. Three main activities are being considered: an arts and crafts workshop, a live music performance, or an international food fair. Which activity do you think would be the best choice to welcome freshmen, and why?

---

### Bảng dàn ý Part 2

| Option | Lý do |
|---|---|
| **Chọn: A live music performance** | - Vibrant atmosphere & Mental health:<br>+ lively melodies, upbeat songs pump up energy and create an exciting welcome vibe<br>+ helps new students forget nervousness, reduce stress, and feel welcomed<br><br>- Easy connection & Making friends:<br>+ young students love music: sing along, dance, cheer together in the hall<br>+ perfect ice-breaker to meet new friends and club members naturally |
| **Không chọn:** | - An arts and crafts workshop: quiet, slow-paced, and limited space; only suitable for a small group rather than hundreds of freshmen.<br>- An international food fair: hard to prepare varied foods, requires huge budget, and takes a lot of time to clean up. |
