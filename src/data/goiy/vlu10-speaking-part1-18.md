### Let’s talk about Happiness & Life Satisfaction
1. What kinds of small things make you truly happy in daily life?
2. Who do you usually share your happy moments and achievements with?
3. Do you believe that money can buy genuine happiness? Why or why not?

| | answer | reason | example (who, what, when, where, how, why) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Enjoying a hot cup of coffee in early morning, listening to acoustic music, and finishing tasks early<br>- A warm meal with family<br>- A pleasant evening breeze on the balcony | -Mindfulness in simple pleasures:<br>• pause from hectic pace, appreciate present moments<br>• small daily achievements generate dopamine and inner peace<br><br>-Zero cost, high satisfaction | - Waking up on Sunday morning without alarm, brewing drip coffee, and reading 20 pages of a favorite novel |
