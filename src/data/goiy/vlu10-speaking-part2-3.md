### Situation: Choosing the Most Essential Soft Skill for Students
A university career center is planning a practical workshop for students and wants to select the most essential soft skill to focus on. There are three options:
- Time management skills
- Study and self-learning skills
- Communication and teamwork skills
Which skill do you think is the best choice for students and why?

---

### Bảng dàn ý Part 2

| Option | Lý do |
|---|---|
| **Chọn: Communication and teamwork skills** | - Social connection & Confidence:<br>+ make new friends easily, speak up with confidence in front of a crowd<br>+ share ideas clearly, listen to others, and avoid unnecessary conflicts<br><br>- Career preparation:<br>+ almost every modern job requires teamwork and talking to customers<br>+ work well with others to finish tasks faster and achieve better results |
| **Không chọn:** | - Time management: students can practice making to-do lists or using calendar apps by themselves at home.<br>- Study and self-learning: students already do self-study every day for exams, so a group workshop should focus on communication. |
