- Encouraging regular daily physical exercise through community sports
- Restricting advertisements of unhealthy junk foods to children

| **Q2** | Cook healthy home meals and limit screen time | - Parents should be good role models by eating vegetables and drinking plenty of water<br>- Do not stock sugary soda and chips in the fridge; take children outdoors on weekends | • Keeping fresh fruits and milk in the fridge instead of canned soda |
