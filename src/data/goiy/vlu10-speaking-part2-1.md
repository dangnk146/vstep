### Situation: Choosing a Gift for a Colleague
You and your colleagues want to choose a meaningful farewell gift for a colleague who is moving to another company. There are three options:
- A book
- A pair of tickets to an event or movie
- A handmade gift
Which option do you think is the best choice and why?

---

### Bảng dàn ý Part 2

| Option | Lý do |
|---|---|
| **Chọn: A handmade gift** | - Mental health & Emotions:<br>+ show care, love, and thoughtfulness of our team<br>+ unique and special: cannot buy it in any normal shops<br>+ bring back good memories of working together<br>+ feel happy, touched, and appreciated when receiving it<br><br>- Strengthen relationships:<br>+ keep in touch and stay connected even when moving to a new job<br>+ put it on the new office desk as a lovely reminder of us |
| **Không chọn:** | - A book: hard to know his/her reading taste; maybe he/she already read it or doesn't like reading.<br>- A pair of tickets: fixed date and time; might be too busy starting the new job to go, so it is a waste of money. |
