### Situation: Ways to Attract Foreign Tourists to Your City
Your municipal authority is seeking effective ways to attract more foreign tourists to visit your city. Three methods have been proposed:
- Promoting tourism through television and radio broadcasts
- Launching viral campaigns on social media
- Organizing an international travel writing contest
Which method do you think is the best, and why?

---

### Bảng dàn ý Part 2

| Option | Lý do |
|---|---|
