### Situation: Choosing Among Three Job Offers
You have received three different job offers and need to make a decision. There are three options:
- A job with a very high salary
- A job that matches your personal interests
- A job with friendly colleagues and a great working environment
Which option would you choose and why?

---

### Bảng dàn ý Part 2

| Option | Lý do |
|---|---|
| **Chọn: Friendly colleagues & great working environment** | - Mental health:<br>+ feel happy, comfortable, and excited to go to work every morning<br>+ reduce work stress, avoid toxic atmosphere and burnout<br><br>- Relationships & Teamwork:<br>+ colleagues are kind, supportive, and willing to help me when I face difficulties<br>+ learn a lot of practical skills from teammates<br>➔ have job satisfaction and stay with the company long term |
| **Không chọn:** | - High salary: usually comes with heavy workload, late overtime, and too much pressure; no time to take care of health and family.<br>- Personal interests: passion alone may fade if the workplace is negative or stressful. |
