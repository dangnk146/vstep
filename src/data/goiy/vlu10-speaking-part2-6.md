### Situation: Choosing a Venue for a Class Reunion
You and your former high school classmates are organizing a reunion party to reconnect after several years apart. There are three options for the venue:
- A cozy family-style restaurant
- A luxury fine-dining restaurant
- A casual café with snacks and drinks
Which venue do you think is the best choice and why?
