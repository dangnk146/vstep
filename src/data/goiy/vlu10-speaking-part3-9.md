# Part 3 - Topic 9: Factors Influencing Job Choice

**Topic:** Key factors when choosing a job or career
- High salary and financial benefits
- Positive and supportive working environment
- Personal interest and passion
- [Your own ideas]

**Follow-up questions:**
1. What types of jobs are young people in your country most attracted to nowadays, and why?
2. Why do many young professionals choose to change jobs frequently rather than staying long-term?
3. What are the main disadvantages or risks of changing jobs too often?

### Bảng dàn ý Part 3 (Phát triển ý chính & Follow-up Questions)

|---|---|---|---|
| **Ý 1** | High salary and financial benefits | - Ensure financial stability:<br>• pay for living costs (house rent, food, bills), support parents, and save for the future<br>• feel secure and independent | • A software developer earning a high salary to buy an apartment and support family |
| **Ý 2** | Positive and supportive working environment | - Improve mental health:<br>• supportive colleagues and kind managers reduce work stress and avoid burnout<br>• feel happy and enthusiastic to go to the office every morning | • An office where coworkers help each other solve problems instead of blaming |
| **Ý 3** | Personal interest and passion | - Maintain long-term motivation:<br>• love what you do => enjoy working, learn faster, and overcome difficulties easily<br>• brings genuine job satisfaction | • A person passionate about food working happily as a chef in a restaurant |
| **Q1** | Technology, digital marketing, and content creation | - Offer high salaries, dynamic environments, and flexible remote work<br>- Young people love creativity and working from home or coffee shops | • Working as a graphic designer, YouTuber, or TikTok content creator |
| **Q2** | Seeking higher salaries and faster promotions | - Staying at one company often results in slow pay raises<br>- Switching jobs helps gain new skills, wider networks, and 20–30% higher income | • A marketing officer moving to a new agency after 2 years to get promoted |
| **Q3** | Unstable resume and starting from scratch | - Employers may view job hoppers as unreliable or lacking patience<br>- Constantly adjusting to new coworkers, bosses, and company rules causes stress | • An applicant with 4 jobs in 2 years being rejected because employers fear quick resignation |
