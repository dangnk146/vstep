# Part 3 - Topic 15: Hiring a Domestic Helper

**Topic:** Benefits and considerations of hiring a domestic helper or housekeeper
- Having more free time to relax and recharge
- Reducing stress and physical exhaustion from household chores
- Maintaining a cleaner and more organized living environment
- [Your own ideas]

**Follow-up questions:**
1. What important factors should a family consider before deciding to hire a domestic helper?
2. If you were temporarily unemployed, would you still consider hiring a domestic helper? Why or why not?
3. Do you think children in families with housekeepers tend to become less independent?
