### Situation: Handling Wrongly Transferred Money into Bank Account
Situation: You unexpectedly receive a large sum of money transferred into your bank account by mistake from a stranger. There are three options:
- Reporting the incident immediately to the police
- Contacting your bank to request an official verification
- Keeping the money in your account and waiting for someone to contact you
Which action would you take and why?
