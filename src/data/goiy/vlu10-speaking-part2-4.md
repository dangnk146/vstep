### Situation: Career Choice for a Teaching Graduate
Your friend has just graduated from a pedagogical university and is considering where to begin her teaching career. There are three options:
- Teaching at an international school in a big city
- Teaching at a local village school in her hometown
- Teaching at a public school in a big city
Which option would you advise her to choose and why?
