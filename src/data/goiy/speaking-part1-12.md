# Topic 12: Traditional Food

1. What traditional Vietnamese dish do you often eat on special occasions?
2. Which Vietnamese dish do foreign tourists like the most?
3. Do you enjoy cooking traditional food at home?

---

### Bảng dàn ý Part 1

| # | Answer (Lựa chọn & Trả lời trực diện) | Reason (Lý do A-R-E) | Example (Ví dụ thực tế) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Bánh chưng and crispy spring rolls (Nem rán)<br>- Essential festive dishes | - Symbol of cultural heritage and family reunion:<br>• made from fragrant sticky rice, mung beans, and pork<br>• cooking and eating together brings warm Tet holiday spirit | • Gathering around the dinner table on New Year's Eve to enjoy warm spring rolls |
| **2** | Phở (beef noodles) and Bánh mì<br>- World-famous street delicacies | - Distinctive, balanced, and fresh flavors:<br>• aromatic bone broth with herbs, or crispy baguette with pâté and fresh herbs<br>• delicious, fast, and very affordable for travelers | • Foreign backpackers queueing up at street stalls in Hanoi Old Quarter to eat Phở |
| **3** | Yes, I love cooking traditional dishes<br>- Especially on relaxing weekends | - Family bonding and healthy eating:<br>• cooking is a fun way to relax after work and preserve family recipes<br>• home-cooked dishes are fresh, clean, and nutritious | • Cooking braised pork with eggs (Thịt kho tàu) or sour fish soup for Sunday family dinner |
